## Findings resolved

Compound ratings, tried-beer and sync-status reads resolved the active account separately for their constituent queries. A second WAL connection could switch A to B in between, mixing A's check-ins or profile total with B's observations or coverage. Three regression cases use real WAL connections and inject an actual committed switch after the first owner read; each failed before the correction. Read transactions now keep one snapshot throughout each compound operation. `addCoverage` also resolves its destination key once for its read/delete/insert sequence.

No new dependencies or public reader signature changes. Optional writer ownership arguments remain a transitional compatibility mechanism; they do not protect asynchronous ingestion by themselves. `isCurrentHistoryOwner` must be checked inside the same immediate transaction as catalog/history mutations.

Catalog-source deletion audit: `mergeIntoCanonical`, pin matching, brewery-alias deduplication and polluted-on-tap cleanup copy every `(telegram_id, account_key, beer_id)` observation before deleting the source. Legacy-card repair delegates to `mergeIntoCanonical`. Canonical non-null ratings win collisions; zero remains a rating. Check-in references are redirected for every owner. A forced deletion failure verifies rollback.

All user-facing repository history reads include the active account predicate. The backfill job intentionally processes every history row; migration and catalog merges intentionally process all owners. These operations are not user-specific history views.
